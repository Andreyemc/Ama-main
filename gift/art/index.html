Art: any picture, cropped to the card (85.6 × 53.98, about 1712 × 1080 px). Keep the corners calm where the logo, “Gift card”, the amount and the name sit. The print tone is worked out from the picture under each of them, so each keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast (the amount 3:1). If the picture is too busy there, Print protection lays the weakest soft shade behind the print that brings it back to the bar. The tiles above show the contrast for each element.

Varnish layer: Colour takes one colour of the art (pick it on the card). Sweep and Speckle are test patterns. File takes your own layer: a PNG of the same proportions, white or opaque where the varnish goes. Distress breaks the edges into screen-print grain, Relief raises the varnish a hair above the card.
